Before you join

A few things to know.

Does joining guarantee work?

No. We review opportunities as they arise and confirm your interest, availability, rates and project eligibility before including your capacity in a bid or dispatching equipment.

Do I need to be a DBE or minority-owned operator?

We welcome qualified independent operators and fleets. Please list any current DBE, MBE, SBE, veteran-owned or other business certifications so we can assess project-specific requirements.

What documents will you need?

Before project award or dispatch, we may request current insurance, applicable operating authority, certification records and equipment details. Requirements depend on the customer and project.

What about the I-10 Capitol Corridor Project?

Beard Logistics is evaluating the hourly trucking scope. Participation depends on the bid package, prime contractor acceptance and agreed terms. We are not representing that a contract has been awarded.
