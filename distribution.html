What we do

Distribution built around the customer contract.

Beard Distribution Group supports recurring supply programs where customers need reliable sourcing, product availability, delivery coordination, documentation and one accountable commercial partner.

We combine supplier relationships with Beard Logistics' transportation, warehouse and contract-management capabilities so customers can buy products without having to manage multiple vendors, deliveries and service issues.

Supplier SourcingIdentify, qualify and manage manufacturers, wholesalers and specialty suppliers.
Contract FulfillmentManage recurring releases, orders, service levels, documentation and invoicing.
Inventory SupportReceiving, storage, cycle control, staging and replenishment where inventory is required.
Local & Regional DeliveryScheduled delivery, routed distribution and urgent replenishment throughout the Gulf South.
Drop-Ship CoordinationSupplier-direct fulfillment managed under Beard's customer-facing relationship when appropriate.
Vendor ManagementPricing, performance, delivery exceptions, compliance and communication handled through one point of contact.
