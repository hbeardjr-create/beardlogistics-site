Core services

Commercial property care focused on consistency.

The initial service offering is intentionally focused on routine grounds maintenance and exterior property appearance. Chemical spraying, pesticide application and other regulated application services are not included in this offering.

Commercial Mowing

Recurring mowing programs built around property needs, seasonal growth and agreed service frequencies.

Edging & String Trimming

Clean curb lines, sidewalks, sign areas, fence lines and detailed trimming around site features.

Blowing & Site Cleanup

Removal of clippings and loose debris from sidewalks, entrances, curbs and maintained exterior areas.

Litter & Debris Removal

Routine pickup of visible exterior litter and service-related debris to maintain a clean customer-facing property.

Landscape Bed Upkeep

General bed cleanup, hand-weeding and appearance maintenance within the agreed scope of service.

Shrub & Hedge Trimming

Appearance-focused trimming and shaping when included in the property scope and service schedule.

Storm Cleanup

Responsive removal of routine storm debris and exterior cleanup support following Southeast Louisiana weather events.

Multi-Site Route Service

Route-based commercial service designed for companies managing several properties across the same regional market.
