Planning your cross docking

What is cross docking?

Cross docking transfers freight from inbound transportation to outbound equipment with limited storage time. If the next truck is delayed, ask about short-term staging and space availability.

What should I send before a truck arrives?

Send freight photos if relevant, piece count, weight, dimensions, inbound and outbound equipment, ETA and release instructions. We confirm an appointment and safe handling before arrival.
