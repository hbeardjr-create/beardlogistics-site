Planning your load shift repair

What should I do when pallets have shifted?

Do not enter or unload unstable freight without an approved handling plan. Send photos and details so our team can review condition, equipment and safe access before scheduling rework.

Can the freight be reloaded after repair?

Reloading can be coordinated after inspection and the agreed rework. Confirm outbound equipment, receiver requirements and freight release authorization.

What photos and paperwork should I send for a shifted or rejected load?

Send condition photos, shipment identifiers and the receiver's rejection reason before scheduling rework. This lets the team review the freight and requested correction together.

  • Photos from a safe position showing the affected pallets, damaged packaging and how the freight sits in the trailer. Do not enter or disturb unstable freight to take pictures.
  • The bill of lading or shipment reference, commodity, pallet count, dimensions and weight per handling unit.
  • The truck's current location, trailer type, driver contact and requested completion time.
  • The receiver's exception notes and any agreed restacking, packaging or redelivery requirements.
  • The contact authorized to approve rework and release the freight, plus the outbound truck or new delivery appointment if known.

Call (225) 346-1658 to coordinate sending photos, or include the written details in a load repair request. Acceptance, equipment and timing require confirmation. If damage is involved, keep the original condition records and follow the carrier's documentation instructions.
