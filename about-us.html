The leadership behind Beard Logistics

A founder who understands the responsibility behind every load.

Henry Beard is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran, entrepreneur and logistics operator who built Beard Logistics from the ground up in Baton Rouge, Louisiana.

Before entering logistics, Henry spent more than 15 years in automotive sales and management. That experience taught him how to lead teams, earn customer trust, manage high-pressure decisions and remain accountable for results.

He carried those principles into transportation. What began with a truck and trailer developed into a multi-division logistics company supporting freight brokerage, transportation management, warehousing, final mile delivery, freight recovery and project logistics.

Henry remains directly involved in customer relationships, pricing strategy, difficult shipments and the daily operating decisions that protect each customer’s freight and reputation.

Founder and company timeline

From service and sales leadership to a growing logistics enterprise.

Each stage added the experience, relationships and operating capability that Beard Logistics brings to customers today.

1990–1994

United States Marine Corps

Henry developed the discipline, leadership and mission-focused mindset that continue to guide the company.

1990s–2010s

Business leadership

More than 15 years in automotive sales and management built a strong foundation in customer service, team leadership and accountability.

2012

First truck and trailer

Henry entered transportation through hands-on operations, learning the business from the driver and customer perspective.

2016

Beard Logistics founded

The company launched in Baton Rouge and expanded into freight brokerage and nationwide transportation solutions.

2018

ACME Baton Rouge operations

Specialized capacity expanded for oil and gas, petrochemical, pipeline and industrial customers across the Gulf Coast.

2019

Brokerage authority expanded

Independent freight brokerage capability created greater flexibility to source equipment and design customer-specific solutions.

2022

Warehouse operations added

The Baton Rouge facility expanded services into storage, cross-docking, transloading, rework and local distribution.
