Questions

Before we work together.

Does registration guarantee a contract?

No. We review potential opportunities and confirm customer requirements, your qualifications, capacity, scope and terms before including you in a bid. Any facilitation fees or commercial terms will be discussed before commitment.

Can I request supplies without joining the contract network?

Yes. Select Supply pricing and share your product list. We will quote available products, alternatives and delivery terms for your review.

What should I send for an accurate supply quote?

Product or brand and SKU, case/container size, monthly quantity and unit, dilution or dispenser requirements, and delivery location. Current price is optional. You may upload a redacted supply list or invoice.

Do I need a particular business certification?

All qualified janitorial companies are welcome. List any current DBE, MBE, WBE, SBE, veteran-owned or other certifications so we can assess project-specific requirements.
