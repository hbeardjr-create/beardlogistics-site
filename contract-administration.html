What we do

Contract management without losing operational control.

Some contracts require more than one specialty provider. Beard Logistics can serve as the accountable administrative prime, program coordinator or management partner when the solicitation and applicable requirements permit that structure.

We organize the people, subcontractors, schedules, documentation, logistics and customer communication behind the work so the contracting organization has one clear point of accountability.

Clear scope. Qualified providers. When licensed or regulated specialty work is required, Beard Logistics coordinates properly qualified providers and does not represent itself as the licensed trade contractor unless appropriately authorized.

Planning your contract administration

What contract administration can you coordinate?

Can you manage more than one service location?

Multi-location requirements can be reviewed as one account. Provide locations, service frequencies, responsibilities and reporting requirements so the scope can be defined.
