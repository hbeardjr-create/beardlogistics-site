What is required before chemical storage is accepted?

Provide the current SDS, packaging, quantities, compatibility information, storage duration and any temperature or regulatory requirements. Product acceptance is confirmed after review.

Can temperature-sensitive chemicals be stored?

The Beard-operated warehouse is not climate controlled. Temperature-sensitive products must be reviewed before acceptance; do not dispatch them without confirmation.
