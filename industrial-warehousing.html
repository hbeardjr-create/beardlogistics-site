Planning your industrial warehousing

Can you handle heavy equipment or machinery?

Send the weight, dimensions, lifting points and center-of-gravity information for review. The Toyota forklift is rated for 5,000 lb at a 24-inch load center; actual safe capacity depends on the load and configuration. Additional equipment must be arranged where required.

Can storage be combined with project staging?

Receiving, staging, counts, scheduled loading and transportation coordination can be scoped together. Confirm delivery windows and handling requirements before arrival.

What should I send for an industrial storage or project-staging quote?

Send a freight list with pallet or crate counts, dimensions and weight per piece, commodity, photos and any special handling needs. Include expected arrival dates, storage duration, how often material will be collected, and the outbound equipment or delivery requirements. For staged project work, add project or work-order references and the order in which material will be needed. These details let us review space, handling and transportation together before confirming acceptance.

How do I plan staged releases when the plant or jobsite is not ready for everything?

Share the required release sequence and receiving windows before the freight arrives. Beard Logistics can scope receiving, counts, storage, staging and scheduled outbound loading together, with transportation coordinated separately as needed. Identify who can authorize each release, the material references to use and the destination contact. If the project schedule changes, confirm the revised release and delivery window before dispatching a truck. For freight moving straight between trucks instead of into storage, see our cross-docking service.
